Craft Stash is a personal craft-supply inventory and project companion for hobby crafters.

It helps you discover:
• What do I have in my craft room?
• Where is it stored?
• Which of my saved projects can I make with my current supplies?
• What ideas could I create with my scraps and remnants?

Features:
• Multi-batch and physical piece tracking with dye lots, remnants, and dimensions
• Exact, approximate, and presence-only tracking modes
• Category-specific templates for Yarn, Fabric, Paint, Beads, Paper, Tools, and Ribbon/Trim
• Hierarchical storage locations (Room → Rolling cart → Drawer)
• Saved project planning with honest requirement coverage states
• On-device deterministic project matching
• Offline barcode scanning with ZXing
• Portable full ZIP backup (with photos) and CSV inventory export
• 100% offline, zero tracking, zero accounts, no ads
